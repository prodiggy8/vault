> [!definition] Cross product
> The *cross product* finds a vector orthogonal to two given vectors. Let $u=\langle u_{1}, u_{2}, u_{3} \rangle$ and $v=\langle v_{1}, v_{2}, v_{3} \rangle$, the cross product is defined as:
> $$u \times v = \langle u_{2}v_{3} - u_{3}v_{2}, u_{3}v_{1} - u_{1}v_{3}, u_{1}v_{2} - u_{2}v_{1} \rangle$$
