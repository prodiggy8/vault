# Vectors in 2D

Quantity that has magnitude and direction.

- The vector from the origin to $(x, y)$ in component form is $\langle x, y \rangle$
- The vector from $(x_{i}, y_{i})$ to $(x_{t}, y_{t})$ in component form is $\langle x_{t} - x_{i}, y_{t} - y_{i} \rangle$
- Magnitude $|v|=\sqrt{ x^2 + y^2 }$
___
**Scalar multiplication:** $k \cdot \overrightarrow{v}$ is a vector with magnitude $|k||\overrightarrow{v}|$ with same direction 
of $\overrightarrow{v}$  if $k>0$ and the opposite direction otherwise.
___
**Addition:**
$$\langle x, y \rangle + \langle u, v \rangle = \langle x + u, y + v \rangle$$
**Triangle Method:** $\overrightarrow{v} + \overrightarrow{w}$ can be constructed by placing the initial point of $\overrightarrow{w}$ at the terminal point of $\overrightarrow{v}$. 

**Parallelogram Method:** Place $\overrightarrow{v}$ and $\overrightarrow{w}$ at the same starting point, draw a parallelogram from the terminal points. The length of the diagonal is the sum.
___
**Subtraction:**

We just define subtraction from the two operations above: $\overrightarrow{v} - \overrightarrow{w} = \overrightarrow{v} + (-1)\overrightarrow{w}$. Graphically, we can draw a vector from the terminal point of $\overrightarrow{w}$ to the terminal point of $\overrightarrow{v}$.
___
Given a vector with magnitude $|v|$ and angle $\theta$ from the positive $x$-axis, its components are:
$$\langle |v| \cos \theta, |v| \sin \theta \rangle$$
___
Unit vectors have magnitude $1$ and can be found as follows: $\frac{\vec{v}}{|\vec{v}|}$.
The standard unit vectors are $i = \langle 1, 0 \rangle$ and $j = \langle 0, 1 \rangle$.
___

Midpoint of $PQ$ is $P + \frac{1}{2}PQ = P + \frac{1}{2}Q - \frac{1}{2}Q= \frac{P+Q}{2}$
# Vectors in 3D

**Right-hand rule:** $z$-axis is parallel to thumb; x-axis is parallel to arm; y-axis is parallel to other fingers.

The **positive direction of rotation** is **counterclockwise**.
___
**Distance between two points:** $d=\sqrt{ (x_{2}-x_{1})^2 + (y_{2}-y_{1})^2 + (z_{2}-z_{1})^2 }$
___
- Plane parallel to $xy$-plane and contains point $(a,b,c)$ is $z=c$
- Plane parallel to $xz$-plane and contains point $(a,b,c)$ is $y=b$
- Plane parallel to $yz$-plane and contains point $(a,b,c)$ is $x=a$
___
A **sphere** with center $(a,b,c)$ and radius $r$ can be represented by:
$$(x-a)^2 + (y-b)^2 + (z-c)^2=r^2$$
___
All other operations extend analogously to the 2D cases.
