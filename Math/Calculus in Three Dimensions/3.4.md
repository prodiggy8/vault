$r(t)$ is position of the object
$v(t) = r'(t)$ is the velocity
$a(t) = v'(t)=r''(t)$ is acceleration
$s(t) = |v(t)|$ is the speed

Example: $r(t) = t^2i + \sqrt{ 5 - t^2 }j$

	$v(t)=2ti - (5-t^2)^{-\frac{1}{2}} \cdot tj$
	$a(t)= 2i - \dots$
