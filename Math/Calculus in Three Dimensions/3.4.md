$v(t) = r'(t)$ is the velocity -> tangent is just unit vector of velocity
$a(t) = v'(t)=r''(t)$ is acceleration
$s(t) = |v(t)|$ is the speed

##### Theorem
The acceleration lies on the plane formed by $T(t)$ and $N(t)$. Furthermore:
$$a(t) = s'(t)T(t) + [s(t)]^2 \kappa N(t)$$
## Tangential and Normal Components of Acceleration

$$a_{T}=a \cdot T = \frac{v \cdot a}{|v|}$$
Notice tangent is just unit vector of velocity

$$a_{N}=a \cdot N = \frac{|v \times a|}{|v|}$$
