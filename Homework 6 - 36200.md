#### Problem 1
###### a)
It’s the mean time all shoppers spend in the store, $\mu$.

###### b)
$$
\begin{gather}
\overline{x} \pm z^* \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{ n }} \\
95\% \implies z* = 1.96 \\
12.5 \pm 1.96 \cdot \frac{5.6}{\sqrt{ 49 }} \\
12.5 \pm 1.568
\end{gather}
$$
Hence, the confidence interval is $(10.93, 14.07)$.

###### c)
Since we have $n\geq 30$, by Central Limit Theorem, we can assume the distribution of the sample mean approximates a normal distribution.

#### Problem 4
###### a)
The percentage of US adults who respond “struggling” to how they feel about their lives, $p$.

###### b)
$$
\begin{gather}
\hat{p} \pm z^* \sqrt{ \frac{\hat{p}(1 - \hat{p})}{n} } \\
0.55 \pm 1.96 \cdot \sqrt{ \frac{0.55 \cdot 0.45}{618}  } \\
\approx 0.55 \pm 1.96 \cdot 0.02 \\
= 0.55 \pm 0.039
\end{gather}
$$
Hence confidence interval is $(0.511, 0.589)$.

###### c)
$np̂ = 618(0.55) = 339.9$ and $n(1−p̂) = 618(0.45) = 278.1$. Both are well above 10, so the  distribution of the sample proportion is approximately normal.

###### d)
$0.57$ falls inside the confidence interval, so it’s plausible as a value for $p$. There isn’t sufficient evidence that the proportion has decreased since 2023.
