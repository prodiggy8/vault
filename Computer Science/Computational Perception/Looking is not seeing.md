> The brain is not an image-processing device. It builds an internal model that is good enough to recognize with, remember with, imagine with, and describe to someone else.


#### Yarbus painting experiment

The questions asked were: free examination, estimate the family’s material circumstances, give the people’s ages, guess what they had been doing, remember the clothes, remember the positions of people and objects, estimate how long the visitor had been away. **Each instruction produced a different path.**

#### Johansson, Holsanova, Holmqvist eye movements in the dark

Subjects fixated while viewing a scene or hearing it described – no eye movements during encoding. Then asked to recall it, they moved their eyes in patterns matching the scene’s spatial layout, in *complete darkness*.

- **Spatial structure exists in the person’s internal representation**
- **Eye movement is not a replay of motor experience**
- **Eye movement may play a functional role in organizing and retrieving visuospatial information**
