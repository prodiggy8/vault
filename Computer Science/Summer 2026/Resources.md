# Resources

Up: [[Fall 2026 Prep]]

## 15-451

**Primary**
- **Jeff Erickson, *Algorithms*** — free PDF, `jeffe.cs.illinois.edu/teaching/algorithms/`. Best free problem sets in existence for this material. Ch. 3 (DP), ch. 4 (greedy), amortized, flows, NP-hardness.
- **Kleinberg & Tardos, *Algorithm Design*** — ch. 7 for flows, and weight the *modeling* sections (§7.5 onward) far above the algorithms. Ch. 8 for reductions.
- **cp-algorithms.com** — segment trees, lazy propagation, string hashing. Implementation-first, which is what you want for the data structures unit.

**Supporting**
- CLRS ch. 17 (amortized analysis) as a backup exposition if the potential method doesn't click
- Mitzenmacher & Upfal ch. 1–4 — if you want the probability rigorous rather than operational
- USACO Guide (Platinum tier) — DP shapes and segment trees, heavy problem volume

**Past course instances** — public, with homework, solutions, and lecture notes. Highest-fidelity practice available.
- `cs.cmu.edu/~15451-f22/`
- `cs.cmu.edu/~15451-f23/`
- `cs.cmu.edu/~15451-f24/`
- `cs.cmu.edu/~15451-s25/`
- `cs.cmu.edu/~yangp/15-451/` (Spring 2026, Liu & Peng)

> [!tip]
> Different instructors, different emphases. Pull midterms from *several* instances rather than doing one instance thoroughly — you want breadth of problem style, not depth on one professor's taste.

## 21-259

**Primary**
- **Paul's Online Math Notes, Calculus III** — `tutorial.math.lamar.edu`. Your spine. Matches the topic list almost exactly, every practice problem fully worked.
- **Stewart, *Calculus: Early Transcendentals*, 8th ed.** — the standard 21-259 text. Ch. 12–16. Use it for problem volume.

**Supporting**
- **APEX Calculus Vol. 3** — `apexcalculus.com`, free, used by some 21-259 sections. Good explanations, and there's a version with rotatable 3D graphs that's genuinely useful for ch. 12 and 15.
- **MIT OCW 18.02 Multivariable Calculus** — problem sets and exams with solutions. Harder setup practice than Stewart. Search OCW for 18.02.
- Past 21-259 exams turn up on instructor pages under `math.cmu.edu` — e.g. `math.cmu.edu/~handron/21_259/info.html`
