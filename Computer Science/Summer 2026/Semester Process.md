# Semester Process

Up: [[Fall 2026 Prep]]

> [!important] Read this honestly
> Prep alone will **not** get you from 15h to 10h on 15-451. Roughly half the reduction comes from these four habits. 10h/week is what a well-organized student in that course actually spends; 15h is usually a symptom of starting late and not going to office hours, and no amount of August studying fixes either of those.
>
> The 21-259 reduction (10 → 7) is different — that one really does come mostly from the [[Week 1 - DP to Reflex|Week 1]] integration drill.

## 1. First-pass every homework problem within 24h of release

Ten minutes per problem. Write down candidate techniques. **Then stop and go do something else.**

Your background processing is free labor, but it only runs on problems you've loaded. Read a problem Monday and the solution frequently arrives Wednesday in the shower, at zero marginal cost. Read it Thursday night and you pay full price for every minute.

This is the single largest time saver on the list.

## 2. Hard time-box: 45 minutes of zero progress → office hours

Not "45 minutes of work." Forty-five minutes of *no forward movement at all*. 15-451 staffs a lot of TAs; the marginal cost of asking is near zero and the marginal cost of grinding alone past that point is enormous.

Grinding feels virtuous. It isn't. It's the most expensive thing you can do in this course.

## 3. Match effort to grade weight

If Fall 2026 keeps completion-graded homework at 5%: write it **correct and legible, not beautiful.**

People routinely burn four hours a week on LaTeX aesthetics for five percent of a grade. That's ~56 hours a semester spent on a fifth of one letter grade. Save the polish for oral HW and exams, where presentation is the thing being assessed.

## 4. Batch-check calculus answers

Do the entire problem set, *then* check. Checking after each problem roughly doubles the wall-clock time through context switching, and it teaches you to rely on immediate feedback you won't have on the exam.

## Weekly rhythm

- [ ] **Sun** — 20 min: skim next week's lecture notes / problem list. Converts lecture from first exposure to review.
- [ ] **Within 24h of release** — 10 min/problem first pass on 451 homework
- [ ] **Mid-week** — real work session, 45-minute stuck rule enforced
- [ ] **Fri/Sat** — 21-259 set in one sitting, timer on, batch-check
- [ ] **Sun** — add rows to [[Technique Index]] and [[DP Problem Log]]

## Tripwires

If any of these are true for two weeks running, something is wrong and the fix is process, not more hours:

- You're starting 451 homework less than 48h before it's due
- You haven't been to office hours
- You can name a technique but couldn't say what problem feature triggers it
- Your [[Technique Index]] hasn't grown
