# 21-259 Prep

Up: [[Fall 2026 Prep]]

## The actual target

This course is throughput-limited. The 6h/week of homework is mostly integral grinding and setup errors. Make the integration automatic and the region-sketching confident, and 6h becomes 3h.

Of the two courses, **this reduction is the reliable one.** 10 → 7 is very likely achievable and most of it comes from a single item below.

## Text

Stewart, *Calculus: Early Transcendentals* 8th ed. is the standard 21-259 text (some sections have used APEX Calculus Vol. 3, which is free). But your **primary spine should be Paul's Online Math Notes, Calculus III** — it matches the topic list almost exactly and every practice problem has a fully worked solution. See [[Resources]].

Chapter map: 12 (vectors) → 13 (vector functions) → 14 (partial derivatives) → 15 (multiple integrals) → 16 (vector calculus).

## The sleeper item

> [!danger] Single-variable integration is the whole ballgame
> Every double and triple integral is nested single integrals. If u-sub, by parts, trig substitution, and partial fractions aren't *automatic*, a problem set that should take 4 hours takes 9 — and you'll blame multivariable calculus for a single-variable problem.
>
> This is [[Week 1 - DP to Reflex|Week 1]]. Do it first.

## Deliberately skipped

Green's theorem, Stokes' theorem, divergence theorem.

Late-semester material, and it makes far more sense *after* lecture. Pre-learning it is the lowest-ROI hour in this plan.
