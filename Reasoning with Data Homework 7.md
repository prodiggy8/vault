### Problem 2

##### a)

Let $p$ be the population proportion of people who get relief from the new formula.
$H_0:p=0.75$
$H_A:p>0.75$

##### b)

$$
\hat{p}=\frac{117}{150}=0.78
$$

##### c)

$$
z=\frac{\hat{p}-p_0}{\sqrt{p_0(1-p_0)/n}}
=\frac{0.78-0.75}{\sqrt{0.75(0.25)/150}}
\approx 0.849
$$

##### d)

$$
p\text{-value}=P(Z\geq 0.849)\approx 0.1981
$$

##### e)

Since $p\text{-value}=0.1981>0.05$, fail to reject $H_0$. There is insufficient evidence that the new formula provides relief to a greater proportion of people than the old formula.
