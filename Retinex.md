Luminance at eye = Illumination * reflectance

For each wavelength

Brightness: perceived luminance

Lightness: perceived reflectance

Weber’s law: just noticeable difference

Perceived intensity grows like the logarithm of physical intensity

White sheet in shadow, grey sheet in sunlight could send same light, yet brain sees one as white and the other as gray. Brain splits the product back into light and paint.

- Light changes gradually
- Paint changes abruptly

Logs because turn the product into sum

1. Compare neighbors by ratio (derivative)
2. Threshold (normalize to 1 the lighting) (threshold)
3. Multiply back up (integrate)

In 2D it uses the Laplacian

For color, apply retinex separately to R, G, B.
